EWB Smart
Plant Pot

[Engineering Analysis] [Siemens NX CAD] [LED Prototyping] [Group Project] EWB University Brief · 2025

A six-person group project designing a smart indoor plant pot for primary schools in Ladywood, Birmingham. The brief, set by Engineers Without Borders, required a low-cost, self-managing growing system suitable for a classroom environment.

The integrated system combines a light sensor, moisture sensor, PID-controlled LED grow light, self-watering reservoir, and adjustable-height frame. My contributions focused on leading the prototyping of LED geometries — designing and testing ring and dome configurations to optimise light distribution across the plant canopy.

I wrote the technical analysis of the light system with PPFD and DLI calculations to validate the LED specification, and built the majority of the CAD assembly in Siemens NX. The PID control diagram was developed alongside the light system analysis to demonstrate the feedback loop between sensor input and LED output.
