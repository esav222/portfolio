ABOUT

I'm a Product Design Engineering student at Loughborough University with a background in hands-on making that goes back to GCSE level, where I built a bent-laminated oak desk lamp using CNC machining, soldering, and craft finishing.

Through A-Level Product Design I developed skills in CAD modelling, design iteration, multi-process prototyping, and electronics integration. At university I've added quantified technical analysis, sensor-driven prototyping, engineering drawing to BS8888, product visualisation, and collaborative project work.

What connects all of this is an interest in understanding how things work and making them real — from the structural problem of counterbalancing a cantilevered lamp, to the PID control loop behind an automated grow light, to the sensor logic that decides when a bathroom needs ventilation.
