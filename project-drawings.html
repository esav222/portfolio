Technical drawings of a motor mount plate and a stepper-motor-driven linear stage, produced to BS8888 (ISO-aligned) standard — the formal language of engineering communication. The package covers a complete part drawing and a full assembly documentation set.

The part drawing (Sheet 1) includes orthographic projections with a section view, complete dimensioning with tolerances, and tapped and countersunk hole callouts. Every dimension serves a purpose and the drawing is readable without ambiguity by any trained engineer.

The assembly set includes a sectioned assembly view showing internal fit and clearance, an isometric, an exploded view with leader lines, and a parts list. Together these represent the standard deliverable for communicating a designed assembly for manufacture.
